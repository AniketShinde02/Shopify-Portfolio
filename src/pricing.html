Common Queries.

Clear answers to help you decide.

What is your typical project timeline?
Shopify Storefronts take 14–28 days. Custom Websites take 4–6 weeks. Full-Stack Web Apps & SaaS platforms take 6–10 weeks. Timelines begin after receiving all required assets (branding, content).
How does the "100% Satisfaction" work?
I present a design mockup within the first 48 hours. If you feel it doesn't align with your brand vision, you can request a full refund immediately—before we move to the development phase.
Do I need to buy a theme separately?
Yes, if you choose a premium Shopify theme. I can work with free themes (like Dawn) or recommend high-conversion premium ones that I'll then customize to match your brand identity perfectly.
Can you help with platform migrations?
Absolutely. I specialize in migrating data, products, and customer profiles from WooCommerce, Magento, or custom-built stacks over to Shopify without losing your SEO rankings.
