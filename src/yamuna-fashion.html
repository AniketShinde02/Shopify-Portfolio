Overview

Yamuna Fashion, a major retailer based in Surat, needed a robust online storefront to bring their curated selection of ethnic apparel (kurti sets, sarees, lehengas) to a pan-India audience.

The platform was built on Shopify with heavy customizations to ensure lightning-fast performance, localized payment gateways, and a seamless mobile-first browsing experience for their diverse customer base. Additionally, I implemented automated WhatsApp flows to handle order tracking and customer support natively.
