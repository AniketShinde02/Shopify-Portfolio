Overview

Makeup By Tipash is a professional business platform built for a bridal makeup artist and beauty brand. The website serves as both a marketing platform and an operational system for managing inquiries, bridal bookings, makeup services, classes, and educational cohorts.

The goal was to create a premium online presence while streamlining customer acquisition and booking management through a custom-built infrastructure.
